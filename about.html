About Us

Nurturing Excellence
Since 1852

St. Thomas SPG Schools is dedicated to nurturing young minds with a balanced blend of academic excellence, moral values, and holistic development. Our experienced educators, safe campus, and student-centered approach ensure every child receives the attention and guidance needed to grow confidently. From Nursery to 10th Class, we prepare students for a successful future.

Founded by Rev. Paran Jyothi in 1852, our institution has long been a pillar of service to humanity. Today, we continue this legacy by fostering consistent self-improvement and inspiring goal-setting in a joyful environment.

Our Foundation

Vision, Mission & Values

👁️

Our Vision

To inspire and empower students to become confident, responsible, and compassionate individuals, equipped with knowledge, values, and skills to succeed in a dynamic world.

🚀

Our Mission

To provide quality education in a nurturing environment, fostering academic excellence, moral integrity, creativity, and lifelong learning to develop well-rounded individuals.

💎

Our Values

Our core values include integrity, respect, responsibility, compassion, and excellence. We strive to instill these principles in every student, guiding them to be ethical citizens.

Our Aim

Towards Excellence

At our school, we are dedicated to guiding students on their journey towards excellence. We believe in helping every student reach their full potential by:

Promoting consistent self-improvement
Inspiring goal-setting and self-motivation
Upholding high academic standards
Active participation in co-curriculars

Nurturing young minds to reach their fullest potential since 1852. Managed by St. Thomas' Tamil Church Society.
