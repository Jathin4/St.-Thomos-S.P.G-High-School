About Us

Nurturing Excellence Since 1852

St. Thomas’ SPG Schools carry forward a proud legacy of education and service rooted in the vision of the St. Thomas’ Tamil Church Society, whose journey began in 1852. For more than 170 years, our institutions have been committed to providing quality education that nurtures academic excellence, character, discipline, compassion and social responsibility. We believe true education goes beyond the classroom, empowering students to grow into confident, responsible and conscientious individuals. The Church leadership and School Management work together to preserve the values and traditions of the institution while responding to the changing needs of education and society. Guided by an elected Board of 18 members in association with the Bishop and clergy, the Society provides responsible leadership and direction to its institutions. Today, St. Thomas’ SPG Schools continue this rich legacy with a commitment to excellence, integrity, inclusiveness and service, inspiring every child to grow in knowledge, character, compassion and conscience. The Management remains committed to preserving the institution’s legacy while embracing innovation and the evolving needs of education.

Vision, Mission & Values

👁️

Our Vision

To inspire and empower students to become confident, responsible, and compassionate individuals, equipped with knowledge, values, and skills to succeed in a dynamic world.

🚀

Our Mission

To provide quality education in a nurturing environment, fostering academic excellence, moral integrity, creativity, and lifelong learning to develop well-rounded individuals.

💎

Our Values

Our core values include integrity, respect, responsibility, compassion, and excellence. We strive to instill these principles in every student, guiding them to be ethical citizens.

Our Aim

Towards Excellence

At our school, we are dedicated to guiding students on their journey towards excellence. We believe in helping every student reach their full potential by:

Promoting consistent self-improvement
Inspiring goal-setting and self-motivation
Upholding high academic standards
Active participation in co-curriculars
