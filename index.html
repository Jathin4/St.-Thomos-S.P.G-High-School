Our Legacy

A Legacy of Shaping Young Minds

1852Established
Nurturing young minds to reach their fullest potential, since 1852.
Read Full Story

St. Thomas' SPG church was established by Rev. Paran Jyothi in the year 1852 solely as a service to humanity for imparting education and building character.

St. Thomas (SPG) schools at Ammuguda, Bolarum, Secunderabad & Trimulgiri were established to educate the children of the society. All the schools are recognised by the Govt of Telangana and today they all are full-fledged English medium schools managed by the St. Thomas' Tamil Church Society, Secunderabad.

From the Church

Church Leadership Desk

St. Thomas' SPG Schools stand on the foundation laid by the St. Thomas' Tamil Church Society, guiding our institutions with the same faith and purpose since 1852. Our church leadership works closely with the school management to ensure every child grows not only in knowledge but in character, compassion, and conscience. Their continued guidance keeps our schools rooted in service to the community. We are grateful for their pastoral care and constant support to our students, staff, and families.
