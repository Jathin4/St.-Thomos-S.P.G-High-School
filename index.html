Chairman's Message

At St. Thomas S.P.G High School, we believe that education is not merely the acquisition of knowledge, but the formation of character. Our institution has been built on the pillars of integrity, excellence, and compassion.

Since 2000, we have remained committed to providing world-class education while staying rooted in Indian values and culture. Every child who enters our doors is treated as an individual with unique talents and potential.

🎯 Vision

To be a centre of transformative education that produces responsible global citizens.

🚀 Mission

To nurture young minds through innovative teaching, strong values, and holistic development.

Academics

Our Curriculum

📘

Primary (I–V)

Foundation years focusing on core literacy, numeracy, curiosity, and creativity through activity-based learning.

📗

Middle School (VI–VIII)

Concept-based learning with emphasis on critical thinking, science, mathematics, and Olympiad preparation.

📕

Secondary (IX–X)

Board-exam focused curriculum with competitive exam coaching for JEE, NEET, and other national tests.

🏆

Olympiad Training

Specialised coaching for Mathematics, Science, English, and Cyber Olympiads at national and international level.

💻

Digital Education

Smart classrooms, coding programs, and digital literacy integrated into every grade.

🎨

Arts & Life Skills

Drawing, music, dance, drama, and life skills as part of the holistic curriculum.
